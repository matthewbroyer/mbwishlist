Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

mbwishlist.online is a free tool built by one person. There's no company, no account system, and no server database behind it. This page explains exactly what happens to your information.

What's stored, and where

Everything you enter (item names, categories, priority, star ratings, cost, quantity, need-by dates, notes, purchase links, and any photo you upload) is saved only in your browser's local storage, a feature browsers provide for websites to save data on your own device. It is not sent to a server, because this app doesn't have one.

The specific browser storage keys this app uses are: wantlist_items_v1 (your list), wantlist_theme (light/dark mode), wantlist_view (grid or table view), wantlist_last_backup (when you last saved a backup), wantlist_privacy_ack (whether you've dismissed the storage notice), and wantlist_onboarded (whether you've seen the welcome screen).

If your browser supports choosing a save location, the app also keeps a reference to the backup file you picked in the browser's IndexedDB storage (database mbwishlist), so "Save backup now" can write to it again. That reference only lets this page write to that one file after your browser grants permission. Browsers never give the page the full folder path.

What this app does not do

  • No account or sign-up, so there's no password or profile to store
  • No server or database. Nothing you type is transmitted or stored outside your browser
  • No analytics, tracking scripts, web fonts, or advertising of any kind. The page makes no requests to outside servers
  • No cookies are set by this app (the storage notice is about local storage, not cookies)

Product links

Purchase links you add point to third-party retailer sites (Amazon, Walmart, and similar, when the app recognizes the link and shows a small label for it). Clicking one takes you to that outside site, which has its own privacy practices and may track your visit there. This app has no partnership with, and gets no data back from, any retailer.

Your data, your control

  • Export: the Data menu saves a copy of your list as a JSON backup or a CSV spreadsheet, any time, to a location you choose (or to Downloads in browsers that don't support choosing).
  • Delete: "Clear wishlist items" removes your items. "Clear all data" wipes everything this app has stored in your browser, including the remembered backup file location. Backup files you saved yourself are never touched.
  • Because nothing exists outside your browser, clearing your browser's site data, switching browsers or devices, or using private/incognito mode also means the data simply isn't there. There's no copy elsewhere to recover it from, other than backups you saved.

Sensitive information

Please don't enter passwords, card numbers, government ID numbers, or other sensitive information. This is a plain local list, not a secure vault, and anything you save stays in your browser's storage indefinitely, in plain text, until you delete it. Backup files are plain text too.

Children's privacy

This app isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age. It doesn't collect information at all, since nothing leaves your browser.
