Privacy & storage

This list saves to your browser's local storage and IndexedDB, not cookies. Nothing is sent to a server or shared with anyone.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

mbwishlist.online is a free tool built by one person. There's no company, no account system, and no server database behind it.

What's stored, and where

Item details are saved in local storage. Photos are saved in IndexedDB on your device. Backup file handles (when supported) are also kept in IndexedDB. Nothing is sent to a server.

Keys used include: wantlist_items_v1, wantlist_theme, wantlist_view, wantlist_last_backup, wantlist_privacy_ack, wantlist_onboarded, and IndexedDB database mbwishlist.

What this app does not do

  • No account or sign-up
  • No server or analytics
  • No cookies set by this app
